# Homepage element-by-element audit

## Visitor job

A first-time Crest visitor should understand the offer quickly, trust the studio, identify the right next step, and reach an estimate or enquiry without reading the complete catalogue on the homepage.

## Placement decisions

| Current element | Why it exists | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Header navigation and booking CTA | Gives global orientation and a persistent conversion route | Keep. The header is the site’s primary wayfinding and should remain visible and legible. |
| Hero eyebrow, headline, supporting copy | States the offer and audience immediately | Keep, but make the message broader than only “protection” so detailing visitors are not excluded. |
| Hero carousel | Supplies visual proof and premium atmosphere | Keep. It is the strongest visual device and is already accessible and pure Astro. |
| Hero note with a second Crest × Rodim lockup | Repeats the brand message already present in the eyebrow and header | Remove. It adds visual weight without adding a decision or proof point. |
| Two hero actions | Separates exploration from action | Keep, but make the estimate the primary action and Rodim PPF the secondary product route. |
| Four proof metrics | Adds credibility after the hero | Reduce to three useful signals. The launch year is not a strong first-visit decision aid and creates a fourth visual block. |
| Three popular-service cards | Helps visitors who do not know the catalogue vocabulary | Keep as the first decision section, with consistent card anatomy and a direct service-detail action. |
| Crest standard editorial section | Explains why the studio is different | Keep after service discovery, because trust is more persuasive once a visitor sees the available care. |
| Three pathway cards for Packages, Protection, Estimate | Routes visitors with different intent | Keep, but frame them as “continue from here” choices and give them more breathing room rather than another catalogue grid. |
| Ceramic image-proof section | Repeats protection after Rodim already leads the hero and the pathway section | Remove from the homepage. The Protection route already owns this depth; the homepage should avoid repeating the same promise. |
| Final contact CTA and direct contact details | Captures visitors ready to enquire without more exploration | Keep as the closing action. |
| Chatbot, back-to-top, sticky booking | Utility and assistance | Keep globally, but do not compete with the hero CTA. |

## Refactored journey

The homepage will flow as: orientation and primary action → concise proof → three clear service entry points → Crest process/trust → three intent-based routes → direct enquiry. This reduces repetition, improves scanning, and keeps detailed pricing and technical protection information on dedicated pages.

## Acceptance criteria

The homepage must remain concise, retain the accessible HeroCarousel, preserve all existing routes and hrefs, keep the primary Rodim PPF positioning, maintain a clear estimate path, avoid decorative numbering, remain responsive at desktop/tablet/mobile widths, and pass the existing check, build, and E2E commands.
